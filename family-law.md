# Family Law Lawyer in India

Family disputes touch the most personal parts of a client's life — a marriage, a child, a home, a parent's welfare. legalgroup's family law advocates handle divorce, mutual consent divorce, child custody, maintenance and domestic violence matters before family courts and district courts across India, with dedicated support in New Delhi.

## What Falls Under Family Law

- Contested and mutual consent divorce under the Hindu Marriage Act, 1955 and the Special Marriage Act, 1954
- Child custody and guardianship under the Guardians and Wards Act, 1890 and the Hindu Minority and Guardianship Act, 1956
- Maintenance and alimony for spouses, children and parents under Section 144 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (formerly Section 125 CrPC), and under personal law
- Protection and residence orders under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005
- Cruelty complaints under Sections 85 and 86 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (formerly Section 498A IPC)
- Adoption, succession and matrimonial property disputes

## How a Family Law Case Typically Proceeds

1. **Consultation** — we review the facts, the marriage or custody history, and any prior filings.
2. **Strategy** — contested vs. mutual consent, interim relief needed, and realistic timelines.
3. **Filing** — petition, supporting affidavits and documentary evidence prepared and filed in the correct family court.
4. **Hearings** — representation at mediation, evidence and argument stages.
5. **Resolution** — decree or order, followed by execution or compliance support.
