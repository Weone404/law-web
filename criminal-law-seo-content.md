## FAQs
*(New section — hub pages with FAQ schema markup are strong candidates for "People Also Ask" placement on broad queries)*

**What does a criminal lawyer do?**
A criminal lawyer advises and represents a person at every stage of a criminal matter — from responding to a police notice or FIR, to seeking bail, defending at trial, and filing appeals if required.

**What is the difference between the old IPC/CrPC and the new BNS/BNSS?**
The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) and Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS), 2023 have replaced the Indian Penal Code and the Code of Criminal Procedure respectively, renumbering and revising several provisions, including those governing bail and anticipatory bail.

**When should I contact a criminal lawyer?**
As early as possible — ideally as soon as you receive a police notice, learn that an FIR may be or has been registered, or are contacted by an investigating agency, since early legal advice affects the options available later.

**Do you handle cases outside Delhi?**
Yes. Our advocate network covers major cities across India, including Mumbai, Bangalore, Chennai, Pune, Kolkata and Hyderabad, in addition to Delhi NCR.

**Can a criminal case be resolved without going to trial?**
In some categories of offences, compounding, quashing of proceedings, or plea arrangements may be available depending on the nature of the offence and the stage of the case. An advocate can assess whether any of these options apply to your matter.
